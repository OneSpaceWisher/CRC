What is CRCBN?

CRCBN (Craniofacial Research Collective Beta Network) is a connected knowledge graph of research papers, clinical data, and biological concepts related to facial growth and health.

How do I access ARGUS?

ARGUS is available exclusively to CRC members. Once you subscribe, you can access the ARGUS workspace directly from the CRCBN dashboard.

Can anyone join?

Yes. While CRC is built for rigorous research, it is open to clinicians, researchers, and individuals who are serious about understanding craniofacial science.
