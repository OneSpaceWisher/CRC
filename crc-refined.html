What is changing at CRC?

The new CRCBN app brings connected research, search, and private collections into one workspace. ARGUS adds an AI-assisted research Journey, and Expedition offers a cinematic way to read chapters alongside their cited papers. These tools are currently in member beta.

Can I use the new app today?

The new app is in a controlled beta. Members who have beta access can use the “Enter the beta” link. Visit the membership page for current membership benefits, or contact CRC with access questions. Membership does not automatically promise access to every beta feature.

How should I use the research?

Read the source material, review the study context, and take uncertainty seriously. AI-generated content and connected records are starting points for investigation, not guarantees of accuracy. CRC is for education and research; speak with a licensed clinician about personal diagnosis or treatment.
